The honest part: risks

Copy trading is not a shortcut to guaranteed profit. Know these before you start.

!

Slippage

Your fill price can differ from the trader you copy, especially on fast moves or thin markets.

!

Late entries

If you mimic after a trader entered, you may buy higher or sell lower than they did, with less upside left.

!

Leverage

Futures use leverage. Losses scale just like gains, and positions can be liquidated.

!

Averaging into losers

Some traders add to losing positions. A great record can hide a big drawdown waiting to happen.

Past performance doesn't predict future results. Nothing on this page is financial advice. Only trade what you can afford to lose.
